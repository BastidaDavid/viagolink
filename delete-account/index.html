Delete your account and your data — ViaGoLink

Operator: Bastida Systems LLC

Support and privacy: bastidasystems@gmail.com

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open ViaGoLink and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation word shown on screen and confirm the deletion.

Deleting your account removes your access, your profile, your public content and your drafts; profile and license files are removed from our active storage. Deletion cancels pending appointments according to their policies.

If you have a Pro subscription purchased through an app store (Apple App Store or Google Play), that subscription is managed by the store: delete or cancel it from your account's subscriptions in that store, because deleting your ViaGoLink account does not cancel it by itself.

If you cannot access the app

Email us at bastidasystems@gmail.com from the email associated with your account, with the subject «Delete my account». We may verify your identity to protect the account before processing the request.

What may be retained

We may retain minimal operations, fraud-prevention or legal-obligation data for as long as needed for those purposes, with limited access. Backups and technical logs follow provider retention cycles. Full details are in the Privacy policy.
